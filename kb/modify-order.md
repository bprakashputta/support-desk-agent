# Changing a pending order

## What can be changed
While an order is **pending** you can change its shipping address, its payment method, or the options of its items (for example size or color). Nothing else about a pending order can be changed.

## Changing items
Each item can be swapped for an available option of the same product. Item changes can be made only once per order, and afterwards the order can no longer be cancelled or modified, so collect every change before confirming. Any price difference is charged or refunded to a payment method you choose; a gift card needs enough balance to cover what is owed.

## Changing the payment method
You can switch to a single payment method that is different from the original one. A gift card must have enough balance to cover the full order total. The original payment is refunded immediately if it was a gift card, otherwise within 5 to 7 business days.

## Changing the shipping address
The shipping address of a pending order can be updated to any address you provide. To change the default address on your profile for future orders, ask us to update your account address.
