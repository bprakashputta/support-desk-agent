# Your account and identity checks

## Why we verify your identity
Before we look up or change anything we verify who you are, using the email on your account or your full name plus zip code. We do this even if you already know your user id. We can only help with the account we verified in a conversation and cannot discuss or change another person's orders.

## Payment methods
Your account can hold gift cards, PayPal accounts and credit cards. Gift cards have a balance that can be used for orders, price differences and refunds.

## Default address
You can ask us to update the default address saved on your profile. This does not change orders that were already placed; a pending order's shipping address is changed separately.
