# Talking to a person

## When we transfer you
Our assistant handles cancellations, returns, exchanges, order changes and account address updates. If your request is outside what it can do, or you ask for a person, it transfers you to a human agent and passes along a summary so you do not have to repeat yourself.

## Large refunds
Refunds above a set amount are reviewed by a team member before they are processed. This usually takes a few minutes during business hours; the assistant tells you when a review is pending.
