# Cancelling an order

## Which orders can be cancelled
Only orders in **pending** status can be cancelled. Once an order is processed (packed for shipping), delivered or already cancelled, it can no longer be cancelled. A delivered order can be returned or exchanged instead.

## Reasons we accept
To cancel, confirm the order id and one of two reasons: "no longer needed" or "ordered by mistake".

## When the refund arrives
After a cancellation the full order total is refunded to the original payment method. Refunds to a gift card are immediate. Refunds to a credit card or PayPal take 5 to 7 business days.
