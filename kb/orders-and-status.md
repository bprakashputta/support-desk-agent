# Order status explained

## Status values
- **pending**: placed but not yet processed; it can be cancelled or modified.
- **processed**: being prepared for shipping; it can no longer be cancelled or modified.
- **delivered**: arrived; items can be returned or exchanged once.
- **cancelled**: cancelled and refunded.
- **return requested** / **exchange requested**: a return or exchange is in progress.

## Times
All times shown on orders are Eastern Time in 24-hour format; "02:30:00" means 2:30 AM ET.

## Products and items
Every product type (for example "T-Shirt") has several items, which are its specific options such as color and size. Product ids and item ids are different numbers.
